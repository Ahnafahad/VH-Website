Before you apply

Eligibility

Must pass at least 5 subjects in O Level & 2 subjects in A Level.

One subject each in O Level and A Level from Business Studies, Accounting, Economics, Mathematics or Statistics qualifies.

Of the best 7 subjects combined, with at least one A Level subject counted: 4 at grade B or higher, 3 at grade C or higher.

Min. GP 3.0 (O & A Level) A = 5.0 B = 4.0 C = 3.5 D = 3.0
